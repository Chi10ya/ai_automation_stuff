18: Understanding RAG (Retrieval Augumented Generation)

        RAG is an advanced technique in the realm of large language models (LLMS) that enhances their capabilities by comning a retrieval system with a generative model

        Retrieval - External source of knowledge and grounded source of knowledge (Statice) are fetched for information
        Augmented - Both external and static knowledge are combined to create an information
        Generation = The learned knowledge is then generated with accuracy and gives context - aware responses.
   
    ** Both NotebookLM and LocalDB feature of GPT4ALL are an RAG tool

        What are the other usecases of RAG
            > As RAG generated dta based on the context and are precise, it well be very helpful to perform operation in Testing such as
                    > Test Plan generation (in our case)
                    > Test case generation (as we have the requirement)
                    > Sometime test estimation (with historical source of information)
